Exeter, England

Exeter is a generally unremarkable town located in the county of Devon. Being in the south west of England, temperature-wise, it is rather mild and rather rainy. If you're somebody who'd enjoy a cloudy day spent wandering about a quaint little town, then Exeter might be the perfect destination for you.

Why visit?

Well, speaking of pottering around damp, stone-laid streets, if you've got a predilection for medieval architecture, then do I have the place for you. The city itself is nearly 2,000 years old, and its historic buildings are bascially its only notable feature. Most famously, Exeter Cathedral, which was built 976 years ago, and is pictured below.
