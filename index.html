Exeter, England

Exeter is a generally unremarkable town located in the county of Devon. Being in the south west of England, temperature-wise, it is rather mild and rather rainy. If you're somebody who might enjoy a cloudy day spent wandering about a quaint little town, then Exeter might be the perfect destination for you.

Why visit?

Well, speaking of gallivanting across damp, stone-laden streets, if you've got a predilection for medieval architecture, then do I have the place for you.
